## Small enhancements beyond the assignment

localStorage persistence and recovery; uncomplete tasks; safe text insertion; accessible field feedback and keyboard focus; reduced motion; no-JavaScript mobile navigation fallback. No backend, accounts, database, or framework is required.

The sample project content must remain honestly labelled or be replaced with real work.
